## Elements

This lens has no fixed menu — it draws on your interdisciplinary knowledge. Choose **7 mental models from 7 different academic disciplines** (e.g. economics, biology, psychology, physics, systems theory, philosophy, engineering, mathematics, sociology, ecology...) that can be used to explore or learn more about the topics, ideas, and situations in the note. Together, the seven should collectively maximize the rate and depth of learning through the wisdom-of-crowds effect.

For each model, name it as **Model Name (Academic Discipline)**.

## Lens rules

- Explain the **unique value** each model provides to the current situation — not a textbook definition but its application here.
- The integration section explains how to **use the models in tandem**, with practical implementation strategies — the synergy is the point.
- Number the models (1–7) in the output for easy reference in follow-up conversation.
