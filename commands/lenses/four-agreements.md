Based on Don Miguel Ruiz's Toltec wisdom teachings. This lens looks for where the agreements are *not yet fully lived*, and what practical realignment would look like.

## Elements

- **BE IMPECCABLE WITH YOUR WORD** — "How does the person's use of language reflect impeccability, or where might they be misusing the power of their word?" Identify instances of negative self-talk or self-criticism; examine how the person speaks about others (gossip, judgment, criticism); map elements of truth-telling versus exaggeration or distortion; explore how language might be used against self or others. *Goal: reveal how greater impeccability with word can bring freedom, self-respect, and more authentic expression.*
- **DON'T TAKE ANYTHING PERSONALLY** — "How might the person be taking things personally or reacting to others' actions as if they were about them?" Identify expressions of emotional reactivity to others' behavior; examine how the person interprets others' actions as reflections on them; map elements of attachment to others' opinions or approval; explore how emotional freedom might transform the situation. *Goal: extract lessons about emotional freedom, personal immunity, and releasing others' projections.*
- **DON'T MAKE ASSUMPTIONS** — "Where might the person be making assumptions rather than seeking clarity through questions and direct communication?" Identify expressions of mind-reading or projecting intentions onto others; examine how storytelling and interpretation occur without verification; map elements of communication challenges or misunderstandings; explore how clarity-seeking might enhance relationships and understanding. *Goal: reveal lessons about clear communication, courage to ask questions, and freedom from assumption-based suffering.*
- **ALWAYS DO YOUR BEST** — "How does the person's approach reflect doing their best, or where might perfectionism, self-judgment, or lack of presence appear?" Identify expressions of self-judgment or perfectionism; examine how presence versus distraction appears in their actions; map elements of effort, intention, and acceptance of limitations; explore how a perspective of 'always doing your best' might transform self-perception. *Goal: extract lessons about self-acceptance, presence-based action, and freedom from self-judgment.*

## Lens rules

- For each agreement showing opportunity: how it is not being fully expressed → specific examples from the note → key growth opportunities → practical applications for better embodying it.
- In the exploration offer, suggest specific **practices** for strengthening adherence to the most challenging agreement.
- This lens pairs naturally with `create/journal-entry` — offer it when a journal note carries emotional charge, conflict, or self-judgment.
