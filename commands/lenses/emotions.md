Based on David Hawkins's Map of Consciousness — a scale that assigns values to levels of consciousness, from the most contracted attractor fields through transitional states into the expanded fields. The scale helps individuals understand their mental and emotional states and offers a roadmap for growth.

## Elements

**The scale:** Shame (20) · Guilt (30) · Apathy (50) · Grief (75) · Fear (100) · Desire (125) · Anger (150) · Pride (175) · Courage (200) · Neutrality (250) · Willingness (310) · Acceptance (350) · Reason (400) · Love (500) · Joy (540) · Peace (600) · Enlightenment (700–1000)

The path runs from very contracted attractor fields — shame, guilt, apathy, grief, fear, desire, anger, pride — through the transitional fields of courage, neutrality, willingness, acceptance, and reason, into the expanded fields of love, joy, peace, and enlightenment. Each level is associated with a specific set of emotions, beliefs, and behaviors.

## Lens rules

- Read the note with attention to the emotions *behind* what is being said, not just the emotion words used.
- Identify the **3 predominant states**, each with gentle evidence from the note explaining why it was identified.
- Emotion names may stay in English for consistency across the archive, whatever the output language.
- Close by suggesting the dominant state as an `emotion/<state>` tag for the note (applied via `/vn-catalog` or on user confirmation) — over time this builds an emotional timeline across the archive.
- For a full journaling breakdown (topics + classified moments + emotional read in one pass), point to `create/journal-entry`, which uses a compact version of this lens.
