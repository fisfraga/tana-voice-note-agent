## Elements

- **ARIES** — initiation, courage, pioneering spirit, self-assertion. Identify initiative, leadership, pioneering energy; courage and assertiveness; new beginnings and breakthrough moments. *Lessons: courage, initiative, self-expression.*
- **TAURUS** — stability, persistence, embodiment, value creation. Identify stability, patience, endurance; physical reality and sensory awareness; resources, values, security; grounding. *Lessons: sustainability, embodiment, lasting value.*
- **GEMINI** — communication, duality, curiosity, mental agility. Identify versatility, communication, exchange; duality and multiple perspectives; information gathering and sharing; intellectual flexibility. *Lessons: effective communication, adaptability, the power of curiosity.*
- **CANCER** — emotional nurturing, security, belonging, intuition. Identify nurturing, care, emotional connection; security needs and belonging; memory, history, emotional intelligence. *Lessons: emotional intelligence, nurturing, psychological safety.*
- **LEO** — creative self-expression, confidence, play, leadership. Identify creativity, playfulness, self-confidence; recognition, visibility, heart-centered action; authentic self-expression. *Lessons: authentic leadership, creative expression, heart-centered action.*
- **VIRGO** — analysis, refinement, service, practical wisdom. Identify detailed analysis, discernment, improvement; service orientation and practical application; systems optimization. *Lessons: practical wisdom, continuous improvement, service-oriented action.*
- **LIBRA** — balance, relationship, harmony, aesthetic awareness. Identify balance, fairness, relationship dynamics; aesthetic considerations and harmony; diplomacy, negotiation, cooperation. *Lessons: creating harmony, balanced relationships, aesthetic coherence.*
- **SCORPIO** — depth, transformation, shared resources, psychic intensity. Identify depth, intensity, psychological insight; transformation and regeneration; shared resources, power dynamics, taboo areas; emotional honesty. *Lessons: transformative processes, psychological depth, authentic power.*
- **SAGITTARIUS** — expansion, meaning-making, adventure, belief systems. Identify expansion, optimism, exploration; meaning-making and belief systems; cultural exchange, education, philosophical inquiry; visionary thinking. *Lessons: finding meaning, expanding horizons, optimistic vision.*
- **CAPRICORN** — structure, mastery, legacy, responsibility. Identify structure, discipline, long-term planning; authority, responsibility, legacy; maturity, achievement, societal contribution; strategic patience. *Lessons: mastery, responsible leadership, enduring structures.*
- **AQUARIUS** — innovation, community, objectivity, future vision. Identify innovation, originality, progressive thinking; community, collectivity, social awareness; technological advancement and paradigm shifts; objective detachment. *Lessons: innovation, collective consciousness, future-oriented thinking.*
- **PISCES** — unity, compassion, imagination, transcendence. Identify compassion, empathy, universal connection; imagination, dreams, inspiration; spiritual awareness and boundary dissolution. *Lessons: unity consciousness, creative imagination, universal compassion.*

## Lens rules

- Frame each archetype as the **energy the moment is calling for** (or overexpressing) — which qualities the situation invites the user to embody or balance.
- In the exploration offer, suggest practical ways to **integrate and balance** the identified energies.
